5G Technology

The fifth generation of wireless technology is revolutionizing connectivity with faster speeds, lower latency, and massive network capacity.

What is 5G?

5G is the fifth generation technology standard for broadband cellular networks, which began worldwide deployment in 2019. It is designed to connect virtually everyone and everything together including machines, objects, and devices.

5G Hardware: Uses UWB (Ultra Wide Band) · Bandwidth 4000Mbps · Smart antennas · Massive MIMO · Beamforming · mmWave · Sub-6 GHz spectrum
5G Software: Software defined radios, encryption, flexibility · Single unified standard across LAN, WAN, WWW, unified IP.

3G: Operator centric  |  4G: Service centric  |  5G: User centric

5G System

The main domains of the 5G system are:
1) RAN (Radio Access Network)   2) Core Network   3) Transport
4) Network Functions & Cloud   5) Management including Orchestration
